Weltraumsknecht = Space Knight (or maybe Space's Knight?)
Based on the landsknecht

Probably a working title

General idea is a roguelike metroidvania—in the vein of Rogue Legacy 2, except without the "Legacy"—in which you obtain and evolve weapons that are very different from each other.

Most recent roguelikes have between-run progression, but I'd prefer to not have that. Excepting a knowledge compendium, and maybe unlocks of different (common) starting weapons.

On hearing the premise, everyone thinks of Dead Cells, which is valid, but now that I've seen some gameplay I don't think it's that similar:
- Dead Cells seems to be a roguelike in structure with metroidvania floors. Weltraumsknecht will be metroidvania in structure.
- Dead Cells has unforgiving fast-paced combat. Our combat will be slower and more aerial.
- Dead Cells has weapons all over the place. Our weapons will be **much** rarer and a distinguishing feature of the run. Also, hopefully, more distinct.
- Dead Cells seems to only have a few types of enemies per area, we'll hopefully have more variety there.
- Dead Cells has distinct areas, but the rooms within the areas largely blend together. We want more distinct rooms (hopefully without losing the area distinctions).
- also our combat will have skill crits as a major feature, which don't exist in Dead Cells
- and we don't have between-run progression, the only unlocks are knowledge (and *maybe* starting weapon choices).

Rogue Legacy 2 is a much closer comparison. (Though I don't want to follow it *too* closely.)

Also, the weapon evolution system is inspired by Vampire Survivors's. (Though it's diverged, but it still carries that core of discovering evolutions by combining weapons with "passives"(now mods).)

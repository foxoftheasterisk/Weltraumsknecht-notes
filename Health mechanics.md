Player starts with... probably 4 hearts? Maybe 5?
(Flavor may be something other than hearts.)

Enemies almost universally do 1 heart of damage per hit. Bosses, particularly late ones, may do 2 with some attacks.

On death, enemies drop a health shard. Collecting some amount of these (8? 10?) restores a heart.

Hidden in the station there are crystal hearts. Each of these heals two hearts; collecting some number (4?) also adds an extra heart.

When a boss dies, it drops a heart container; this fully heals and adds an extra heart.

Partial hearts (of damage) are probably not a thing. If they are, it's probably like, armor that causes you to take 3/4 heart instead of 1.

These are *probably* all the ways to restore health. I considered others but this seems like enough.

As you explore the station you will discover enemies. You do not technically have to fight them but it makes the station easier to traverse safely, and you will gain rewards from doing so (small amounts of health recovery, and also "coins" if we figure out a use for them).

Each room determines how many enemies appear in it as well as what class of enemies. Which enemies are in the class is determined by the area and (if applicable) the difficulty intended. (Which may be determined by both map generation and difficulty setting.)

Once an enemy is killed, it is gone forever.

I don't know if "coins" is the best way to do it but I think there should be some reward for defeating harder enemies, especially doing so earlier. Having enemies drop scaling amounts of currency seems like the obvious way.

Enemy health scales with areas. At first, the typical amount is between 10 and 20—that is, it takes two standard hits or one crit with a typical weapon.
Some will be closer to 10 so that a damage increase makes it a one-shot, some closer to 20 to continue to reward crits.

Having multiple (2?) levels of the same enemy is a nice feature I think. Allows us to scale them (Thus making easier or harder versions of the same area fairly easily) and when the player encounters the higher level one, they have some idea what it does from the lower level.

RL2's method seems smart: a level one enemy has one attack, level two has an upgraded version of that and a variant.
